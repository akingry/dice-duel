All sound effects in this folder are recordings by Kenney (www.kenney.nl),
released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0, public domain):
  - Casino Audio      (dice shakes, dice grabs)          kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
  - Impact Sounds     (wood/metal/soft impacts)          kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  - RPG Audio         (handful of coins)                 kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
Dice-throw recordings as distributed with Tabletop Club (github.com/drwhut/tabletop-club),
also credited to Kenney. Converted to mono MP3 and trimmed of leading silence.

harp_*.mp3: Folk Harp, single notes, from the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)
by Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner, released under CC0 1.0 (github.com/sgossner/VCSL).
Trimmed to 2.6 s with a fade, converted to mono MP3.
